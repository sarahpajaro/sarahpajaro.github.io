MY APPROACH

From raw information
to a clearer picture.

My projects follow a path from collecting information to asking what it can tell us. I work with annual reports and earnings calls, exploring how financial language can be organized, measured, and classified.

Alongside text mining, I explore machine learning through hands-on projects, including building a neural network for handwritten digit recognition. Each notebook captures the process: the code, the experiments, and the results.

This portfolio brings those projects together, with a look at both how they were built and what their saved results show.
