Instructions

Read a company index, select the first 100 matching 10-K filings while excluding 10-K/A amendments, and download the filing text with identifying metadata.

Running the notebook

A company.idx file, requests, a writable download folder, and internet access. Update the paths and SEC User-Agent with your own valid research contact before running.

Open the original notebook in Jupyter or Google Colab. Review the file paths and dependencies, then run the cells in order. External datasets and Google Drive files referenced in the notebook are not included in this portfolio.
